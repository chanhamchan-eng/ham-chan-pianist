Ham Chan is a Hong Kong pianist whose work moves between solo performance, chamber music, recording and artistic collaboration.

His recording Masks under the Mask explores the inner worlds of Robert Schumann. His performances and projects bring together repertoire, collaborators and audiences across the United States and beyond.
